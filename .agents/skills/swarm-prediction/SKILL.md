# Swarm Prediction & Scenario Simulation Engine

A multi-agent swarm intelligence engine integrated into Jarvis, modeling complex futures through simulated multi-persona interactions.

## When to use
- **Scenario Forecasting:** Simulating the impact of breaking news, macro policies, tech releases, or corporate decisions.
- **Sentiment & Public Opinion Dynamics:** Modeling how different social, technical, or economic groups react, debate, and polarize over time.
- **Risk & Outlier Discovery:** Uncovering unexpected second-order consequences and emergent behaviors through autonomous agent debates.

## Architecture & Workflow

1. **Seed Ingestion:**
   Extracts core entities, constraints, and conflicting factors from the prompt, documents, or data inputs.

2. **Swarm Generation:**
   Constructs a balanced micro-society of specialized personas with distinct motivations, cognitive biases, and memory anchors:
   - *Advocates / Early Adopters:* High optimism, focus on growth opportunities.
   - *Skeptics / Regulators / Critics:* Focus on compliance, risks, vulnerabilities, and failure modes.
   - *Neutral Analysts / Observers:* Data-driven, tracking metric shifts and probabilities.
   - *End Users / General Public:* Practical adoption concerns, cost-benefit trade-offs.

3. **Multi-Round Deliberation (Interaction Sandbox):**
   Agents exchange arguments, react to peer statements, revise stances based on evidence, and form alliances or dissenting factions.

4. **Synthesis & Predictive Reporting:**
   A synthesizing controller analyzes consensus trends, sentiment trajectories, and key branch points to output:
   - **Executive Forecast:** Most likely primary outcome (with confidence level).
   - **Alternative Branch Scenarios:** Black swan / worst-case / best-case pathways.
   - **Critical Pivot Drivers:** The specific triggers that decide which branch unfolds.
   - **Actionable Recommendations:** Strategic decisions or hedges for the user.
